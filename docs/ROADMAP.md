# Qaventra roadmap: FlowHCM module parity

Source: the 18 FlowHCM 5.10 user manuals (Asset, Attendance, Desk, Employee, Separation, Expense, Helpdesk, Leave, Letter,
Manpower Budgeting, Onboarding, Organization, Payroll, Performance, Recruitment, Scheduled Alert, Scheduled Report, Travel).
Goal: every feature they list, built cleaner and more modern (self-service first, mobile-friendly, live previews, fewer forms).

Status: ✅ done · 🟡 partly · ⬜ not started

## Phase 0: shared engine (every module uses these)

The manuals repeat the same five patterns in almost every module. Build once, reuse everywhere.

| Piece | What it gives every module | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Approval workflows | Per request type: auto-approve, line manager, manager's manager, HR, specific employee, in any order (up to 5 steps); approve/reject with notes; one "Approvals" inbox; timeline on every request | ✅ for leave and expenses; each new module adds its request type |
| Attachments | Files on leave requests and separations, employee document folder with type and expiry date; PDF/images/Word/Excel up to 5 MB; open in browser | ✅ (expense receipts kept on claim items) |
| Notifications | In-app bell (refreshes every minute) + email via SMTP for approvals needed, decisions, resignations, letters, payslips; per-user email switch | ✅ (email needs Smtp settings; editable templates later) |
| Excel import/export | Templates, row-by-row check, all-or-nothing import for employees and attendance; exports for employees, attendance, leave, expenses, payroll (bank-ready) | ✅ (more imports per module as they land) |
| Reports | "View report" on every grid: filter, print, export | ⬜ |

## Done since (approvals and reports)

- **Approvals:** HR approves over the chain with a reason (shown as "forcefully approved"); approvers delegate their approvals (all or chosen request types) to a colleague for a period, HR can set one up for anyone, the delegate sees them in their inbox and decides "for" the approver; one decide endpoint for every request type.
- **Reports catalog:** 18 reports in one place (employees, headcount, joiners and leavers, birthdays, documents, daily attendance, attendance register, late arrivals, leave balances, leave taken, salary sheet, bank advice, income tax, EOBI, expense claims, pending approvals, training, trips) with period/month/year/date, department, employee and include-left filters; totals; Excel with real numbers; print/PDF with company header and up to five signature lines; managers get team reports without pay.

- **Payroll depth:** salary revisions for one or many people (percent or amount, with or without allowances, from a date, preview with cost impact, raises loaded from a performance cycle, history, employee told); monthly allowances and deductions with start and end months; salary holds (left out of the bank advice until released); leave encashment (basic or gross ÷ 30, off the balance, paid with the month); optional payroll approval by the company admin (submit, approve or send back, reprocessing resets it), and marking a finalized month as paid.

- **Leave and attendance rules:** reason types per leave type (day limit, document needed; a reason must be chosen when a type has them); sandwich rule (offs and holidays inside a request count); document required beyond N days, enforced before approval; opening balances for everyone in one grid (brought forward and adjustments with a note); excusing late arrivals and half days through their own approval chain, with a monthly quota and time limit, turning the day into present so no late penalty or half-day deduction applies.

- **Employee file:** company lists (divisions, sub-departments under departments, grades, cost centers, regions/stations, employee groups, exit types) with codes and heads; employee file with father name, nationality, religion, CNIC/passport/visa/licence numbers and dates, NTN, EOBI and social security numbers, health insurance, bank title and branch, confirmation and contract end, punch code, exit type and reason, do-not-hire list (blocks re-hire); dependants and nominees with shares, qualifications and previous jobs (employees keep their own); filters by group on the employee list and every report; new reports (employee file, dependants, qualifications, experience); file expiry dates feed the expiring documents report and alert.

- **Attendance depth:** shift flag rules (early-leave grace, half-day and short-day marks, break, earliest check-in) with late and early minutes on every day and a short-day flag; excuses also cover short days and early leaves; holidays as public or special, limited to regions or a religion, with people kept working; requests to move weekly days off for a period (roster rewritten on approval); penalty rules (late, early leave, half day, short day, absent, missing check-out; every N with free ones and minimum minutes; pay or leave, leave falling back to pay) shown in the monthly summary, on the payslip, and taken off leave balances when the month is closed (given back on reopen); per-person check-in rules (web, phone, anywhere, office network IPs, a personal location); early leavers and penalties reports.
- **Projects and timesheets:** projects with client, manager, dates, budget hours, billable, team with hourly rates (or open to all) and tasks with assignee, due date and estimate; weekly timesheets by project and task with notes, compared against attendance hours, submitted through their own approval chain, locked once approved; hours and cost against budget per project and task; project hours report.

- **Shared engine:** requests on someone's behalf (HR for anyone, managers for their team: leave, attendance corrections, expense claims, comp-off, overtime and advance), shown as "filed by" in the inbox and timeline; access templates (sets of modules) that limit an HR user to some modules or open a module's screens to a manager or employee, enforced on every HR and approver endpoint and in the menu; suggestion box with categories and reviewers, anonymous option, statuses and replies; change history on employees and settings records from the audit log; quick search (Ctrl+K) over every screen and people, with pinned favourites.

- **Payroll funds and tax:** provident fund (member and company percent of basic or gross, vesting years, contributions posted when payroll is finalized, opening balances, profit sharing, vested balance), gratuity (days per year of service, minimum years, on basic or gross, opening and payments), withdrawals from either through their own approval chain and paid with salary; tax adjustments per tax year (previous employer's salary and tax, credits, other income) that switch the person to a projected-year tax; commission as a pay item; salary paid by bank, cash or cheque (bank advice only for bank, a cash/cheque sheet with signatures); cost center splits; employer social security (percent and wage ceiling); overtime rates for holidays and days off with minimum and rounding; un-marking a month as paid; reports for social security, cash and cheque, cost by cost center, tax certificate, provident fund, gratuity and commission.

- **Expense depth:** expense types worked out by amount, rate × units or rate × distance between cities (distance list, either way round), with per-item and yearly limits, receipt rule, grade restrictions and grade-specific rates and limits; the claim form shows each type's rate and what is left this year; expense advances through their own approval chain, paid with salary, claims set against them (not paid twice), closing recovers what was not spent.

- **Separation depth:** clearance templates with up to ten levels and an owner per item (owners clear their items from "Exit clearances"; a level opens once earlier ones are cleared); exit interview templates (ratings, yes/no, text) answered by the leaver or the panel, with a panel, a time and private notes; final settlement sent through its own approval chain (completion waits, changes need a new approval) and marked paid by bank, cheque or cash. Approval chains can now be sent again after a rejection or change.

- **Letters depth:** templates that need approval (through the letter approval chain, hidden until approved), self-service letters employees request with a reason, templates issued automatically on events (offer to a candidate, hire, confirmation, transfer or promotion, salary revision with the new salary and date, completed exit), candidate offer letters printable from the application, page margins for printed letterheads and a footer, new placeholders (confirmation date, effective date, candidate name, job, offered salary and joining date).

- **Recruitment depth:** candidate status page from the link in their emails (applications, interviews, offer with accept or decline, offer letter, withdraw); shortlisting score per job (experience, expected salary, city, keywords) with reasons; interview templates and scorecards with marks per question (API).

## Where we aim to beat FlowHCM

- Mobile-first self-service (PWA): check-in, leave, claims with camera receipts, payslips, letters.
- Live previews everywhere (letters, settlement, payroll) instead of submit-then-check.
- One approvals inbox across modules instead of an approvals tab per module.
- Payroll pulls approved items (expenses, overtime, loans) automatically.
